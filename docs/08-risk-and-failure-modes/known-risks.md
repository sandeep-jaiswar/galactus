# Galactus — Regime Breakdown

## Purpose
This document lists **known regime breakdown patterns** and their implications.

---

## Breakdown Scenarios

### 1. Liquidity Mirage
- Apparent liquidity vanishes under stress
- Signals must degrade immediately

### 2. Derivatives Inversion
- Cash leads derivatives temporarily
- Derivatives-based signals must suppress

### 3. Participation Flip
- Retail disengagement or institutional withdrawal
- Crowd-based inference invalidated

- Confidence suppression
- No forced reclassification
