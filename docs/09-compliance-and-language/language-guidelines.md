# Galactus — Language Guidelines

## Purpose
Prevent actionable or persuasive language.

---

## Forbidden Language

- Buy / Sell
- Bullish / Bearish
- Entry / Exit
- Target / Stop

---

## Allowed Language

- Pressure
- Constraint
- Risk
- Uncertainty
- Regime

---

## Tone

- Neutral
- Analytical
- Conservative

---

## Final Statement
**Language shapes liability.**
