# Galactus — Language Guidelines

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines **allowed and forbidden language** for all Galactus outputs, documentation, and communication.

It exists to:
- Prevent actionable or persuasive language that could be construed as investment advice
- Maintain clear boundaries between inference and recommendation
- Protect against regulatory misinterpretation
- Ensure consistency across all system outputs
- Preserve the non-advisory nature of Galactus

**Language shapes liability.** Every word choice matters.

This document is part of the locked vision framework. See [`VISION_LOCK.md`](../../VISION_LOCK.md) for enforcement policy.

---

## Core Principle

Galactus provides **structural inference about capital behavior**, not actionable investment advice.

All language must:
- Describe market structure and constraints
- Avoid implying recommended actions
- Preserve analytical distance
- Acknowledge uncertainty explicitly
- Focus on capital mechanics, not price outcomes

---

## Forbidden Language Categories

The following language categories are **permanently prohibited** in all Galactus outputs, APIs, documentation, and communication.

### Category 1 — Trading Actions (Hard Forbidden)

**Never use:**
- Buy / Sell
- Long / Short
- Enter / Exit
- Add / Reduce
- Open / Close (positions)
- Accumulate / Distribute
- Scale in / Scale out
- Take profit / Stop loss
- Execute / Trade
- Position sizing recommendations

**Why Forbidden:**  
These terms directly imply actionable trading instructions. They cross the line from analysis to advice.

**Violation Risk:** High — Regulatory interpretation as investment advice

---

### Category 2 — Directional Bias & Predictions (Hard Forbidden)

**Never use:**
- Bullish / Bearish
- Uptrend / Downtrend (as recommendation)
- Overbought / Oversold (as signal)
- Strong buy / Strong sell
- Positive / Negative outlook (on price)
- Price target / Target price
- Expected return
- Upside / Downside potential (as recommendation)
- Will rise / Will fall
- Should increase / Should decrease
- Price forecast / Price projection

**Why Forbidden:**  
These terms express directional bias and price predictions, implying that action should be taken based on expected price movement.

**Violation Risk:** High — Implies price prediction and directional advice

---

### Category 3 — Timing & Urgency (Hard Forbidden)

**Never use:**
- Now is the time to...
- Urgent / Immediate action
- Don't miss...
- Last chance...
- Act now
- Window closing
- Opportunity fading
- Time to act
- Before it's too late
- Get in / Get out

**Why Forbidden:**  
Creates false urgency and implies time-sensitive action. Manipulative and advisory in nature.

**Violation Risk:** Critical — Implies immediate action required

---

### Category 4 — Performance Promises (Hard Forbidden)

**Never use:**
- Guaranteed returns
- Expected profit
- Alpha generation
- Outperformance guaranteed
- Risk-free
- Sure thing
- Can't lose
- Will beat the market
- Proven returns
- Consistent profitability
- Reliable gains
- Assured outcomes
- Performance warranty

**Why Forbidden:**  
Makes promises about future outcomes that cannot be guaranteed. Creates false expectations and legal liability.

**Violation Risk:** Critical — Legal and regulatory violation

---

### Category 5 — Personalized Advice (Hard Forbidden)

**Never use:**
- You should...
- We recommend...
- Best for your portfolio...
- Ideal for your situation...
- Suited to your risk profile...
- Right for you...
- Custom advice
- Personalized strategy
- Tailored recommendation

**Why Forbidden:**  
Crosses into personalized investment advice territory, which requires regulatory authorization and fiduciary responsibility.

**Violation Risk:** Critical — Constitutes investment advice

---

### Category 6 — Emotional & Persuasive Language (Forbidden)

**Avoid:**
- Exciting opportunity
- Amazing setup
- Perfect conditions
- Incredible signal
- Fear (as call to action)
- Greed (as motivator)
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Panic (as trigger)
- Euphoria
- Desperation

**Why Forbidden:**  
Emotional language manipulates decision-making and obscures rational analysis. Incompatible with neutral inference.

**Violation Risk:** Medium — Implies persuasion rather than information

---

### Category 7 — Certainty & Guarantees (Forbidden)

**Avoid:**
- Definitely / Certainly
- Always / Never (about future)
- Guaranteed
- Assured
- Without doubt
- Absolutely
- Will happen
- Cannot fail
- Risk-free
- Sure to occur
- Promised outcome

**Why Forbidden:**  
Markets are inherently uncertain. Claims of certainty are false and create liability.

**Violation Risk:** High — False representation of certainty

## Allowed Language Categories

The following language categories are **permitted and encouraged** for describing Galactus outputs.

### Category 1 — Structural Inference (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Capital pressure
- Forced flow
- Structural constraint
- Positioning imbalance
- Liquidity condition
- Institutional obligation
- Hedging requirement
- Delta exposure
- Open interest decay
- Rollover pressure
- Basis convergence
- Settlement mechanics
- Regulatory constraint
- Margin requirement

**Why Allowed:**  
Describes objective market structure and capital mechanics without implying action.

**Examples:**
- "High rollover pressure detected in near-term contracts"
- "Institutional hedging obligations increase at this strike"
- "Basis convergence creates structural pressure on calendar spreads"

---

### Category 2 — Regime Classification (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Regime shift
- Structural regime
- Stability classification
- Confidence regime
- Market microstructure state
- Liquidity regime
- Volatility regime
- Constraint environment
- Pressure regime

**Why Allowed:**  
Classifies market state objectively without prescribing action.

**Examples:**
- "Market entered low-confidence regime"
- "High-pressure regime with elevated structural constraints"
- "Stable liquidity regime observed"

---

### Category 3 — Uncertainty & Degradation (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Confidence degradation
- Increased uncertainty
- Signal instability
- Data quality deterioration
- Inference unreliable
- Structural ambiguity
- Regime uncertainty
- Measurement noise
- Insufficient data
- Inconclusive evidence
- Cannot determine
- Explanation unavailable

**Why Allowed:**  
Explicitly acknowledges limitations and uncertainty. Critical for honest communication.

**Examples:**
- "Confidence degraded due to data sparsity"
- "Inference unreliable in current regime"
- "Insufficient evidence for structural conclusion"

---

### Category 4 — Measurement & Quantification (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Measured at X%
- Observed pressure of Y
- Detected at confidence level Z
- Quantified constraint
- Calculated exposure
- Derived pressure metric
- Measured intensity
- Normalized magnitude
- Relative measurement
- Absolute magnitude
- Statistical significance
- Confidence interval

**Why Allowed:**  
Objective quantification without implication of action.

**Examples:**
- "Capital pressure measured at 72% of historical range"
- "Detected constraint at 0.85 confidence level"
- "Observed positioning asymmetry of 2.3 standard deviations"

---

### Category 5 — Conditional & Contextual Analysis (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Given current structure...
- Under these conditions...
- If constraints persist...
- Assuming stable regime...
- In this context...
- Subject to...
- Conditional on...
- Dependent on...
- Contingent upon...
- In the presence of...

**Why Allowed:**  
Provides context and acknowledges dependencies without prescribing action.

**Examples:**
- "Given current OI structure, rollover pressure may intensify"
- "If volatility regime persists, hedging costs remain elevated"
- "Conditional on stable liquidity, basis convergence expected"

---

### Category 6 — Historical & Empirical Reference (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Historically observed
- Empirically documented
- Previously detected
- Similar episodes showed...
- Past occurrences indicate...
- Statistical tendency
- Historical precedent
- Documented behavior
- Observed pattern
- Recorded instance

**Why Allowed:**  
References past evidence without guaranteeing future outcomes.

**Examples:**
- "Historically, this pressure configuration preceded settlement dislocation"
- "Similar constraint patterns observed in Q3 2023"
- "Empirically, regime shifts of this magnitude required 5-7 days to stabilize"

---

### Category 7 — Mechanism Explanation (Encouraged)

**Use:**
- Market mechanics
- Constraint propagation
- Force transmission
- Causal relationship
- Structural dependency
- Mechanical linkage
- Propagation mechanism
- Forcing function
- Constraint binding
- Obligation trigger
- Hedging cascade
- Liquidity spiral

**Why Allowed:**  
Explains how markets work without predicting outcomes or recommending actions.

**Examples:**
- "Delta hedging mechanically transmits pressure to spot markets"
- "Settlement convergence forces basis to narrow through arbitrage"
- "Margin requirements propagate constraint across related instruments"

## Tone Requirements

All Galactus communication must maintain:

### Neutrality
- Avoid emotional coloring
- Present inference without enthusiasm or alarm
- Acknowledge competing interpretations
- Avoid loaded terminology

### Analytical Precision
- Use specific, technical language
- Quantify when possible
- Define terms explicitly
- Avoid ambiguity

### Conservative Stance
- Acknowledge uncertainty
- Highlight limitations
- Avoid overconfidence
- Prefer "insufficient evidence" to speculation

### Mechanical Focus
- Explain market structure
- Describe capital constraints
- Reference forcing mechanisms
- Ground in observable behavior

## Edge Cases & Clarifications

### Allowed with Qualification
Some terms are acceptable only with proper qualification:

**"Pressure" vs "Opportunity":**
- ✅ "Elevated capital pressure detected"
- ❌ "Opportunity to buy based on pressure"

**"Risk" vs "Risk Management":**
- ✅ "Elevated structural risk in this regime"
- ❌ "Reduce risk by selling positions"

**"Expectation" vs "Expected":**
- ✅ "Structural expectation of basis convergence" (mechanical)
- ❌ "Expected to generate positive returns" (prediction)

**"Signal" vs "Actionable Signal":**
- ✅ "Structural signal detected"
- ❌ "Strong buy signal"

**"Trend" vs "Trending":**
- ✅ "Observed directional pressure over 5 days"
- ❌ "Strong uptrend, buy now"

## Output Examples

### ❌ Forbidden Output Example
```
Strong BUY signal detected! Price is oversold and ready to bounce.
This is an excellent opportunity - don't miss it!
Target price: ₹250, Stop loss: ₹180
Expected return: 25% in 30 days
Act now before the window closes!
```

**Violations:**
- Trading action (BUY)
- Directional bias (bounce)
- Urgency (don't miss, act now)
- Price target (₹250)
- Performance promise (25% return)
- Timing pressure (window closes)

---

### ✅ Allowed Output Example
```
Structural Analysis: NIFTY DEC Futures

Capital Pressure: 73rd percentile (historical range)
Confidence: 0.72 (moderate degradation from data sparsity)

Regime: High-pressure, stable liquidity

Observed Constraints:
- Near-term OI concentration at 19,000 strike (68% of total)
- Rollover pressure estimated at 2.1σ above normal
- Delta hedging obligations elevated

Mechanistic Context:
Given current OI structure, settlement convergence may
propagate constraint through calendar spreads. Historical
episodes with similar configuration showed pressure
persistence for 3-5 days (median).

Uncertainty Factors:
- Foreign flow data delayed 24h
- Volatility regime classification ambiguous
- Insufficient data at far strikes

Confidence Degradation Triggers:
- Regime shift detection
- Data quality deterioration
- Structural assumption violation

Inference Valid Until: Settlement date or regime shift
(whichever occurs first)

Disclaimer: This output provides structural analysis only.
It does not constitute investment advice or trading
recommendation. Capital allocation decisions require
consideration of factors outside Galactus scope.
```

**Compliant because:**
- Describes structure, not actions
- Quantifies confidence explicitly
- Acknowledges uncertainty
- Explains mechanics
- Includes clear disclaimer
- Avoids directional predictions
- No timing pressure

## Legal & Regulatory Context

### Why This Matters

**Regulatory Liability:**  
Investment advisors are regulated entities requiring licensing, fiduciary duty, and compliance infrastructure. Advisory language can trigger regulatory classification.

**Legal Liability:**  
Actionable advice creates implicit duty of care. If users act on apparent recommendations and lose money, language becomes evidence of advisory intent.

**Compliance Burden:**  
Advisory classification requires ongoing compliance, reporting, and regulatory oversight that fundamentally changes Galactus's operational model.

**Preserve Core Identity:**  
Galactus is an **inference engine**, not an **advisory service**. Language must reflect this distinction consistently.

### Safe Harbor Requirements

To maintain safe harbor as informational (not advisory):
- Outputs must be **general**, not personalized
- Information must be **objective**, not persuasive
- Content must be **educational**, not directional
- Tone must be **neutral**, not emotional
- Recommendations must be **absent**, not implied

This document provides the language framework to maintain safe harbor status.
